## 🌍 The Problem
Globally, over 1.3 billion tons of food are wasted annually, contributing heavily to environmental degradation and economic loss. A significant portion of this waste occurs at the household level, where consumers often lack the culinary knowledge to transform mismatched leftover ingredients into cohesive meals or struggle with proper food storage. This inefficiency inflates household grocery bills and directly contradicts global sustainability targets.

## 💡 The Solution
**FoodSaver AI** is an intelligent web application designed to combat household food waste. It empowers users to maximize their existing kitchen inventory by transforming leftover pantry items into gourmet meals. 

By making sustainable cooking effortless, FoodSaver AI helps consumers lower grocery costs, adopt eco-conscious habits, and actively participate in achieving UN SDG 12.

## ✨ Key Features

*   📸 **Smart Ingredient Detection:** Upload a photo of your fridge or pantry. The app uses multimodal computer vision (utilizing TensorFlow.js and simulated backend models like IBM Granite Vision) to extract ingredient entities.
*   🍳 **Generative Culinary Engine:** A dynamic algorithm acts as a virtual chef, categorizing detected ingredients to synthesize customized, step-by-step recipes (e.g., Curries, Soups, Salads, Desserts).
*   🥑 **Nutritional Estimations:** Automatically calculates estimated macros (Calories, Protein, Healthy Fats) based on the detected ingredients.
*   ♻️ **Zero-Waste & Storage Tips:** Provides tailored, ingredient-specific preservation protocols (e.g., dehydrating tomato skins, freezing ginger) to extend lifespans and foster a "Zero Scrap" kitchen philosophy.
*   🎨 **Glassmorphism UI:** A beautiful, responsive, and intuitive user interface.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
*   **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
*   **Machine Learning (Vision):** TensorFlow.js & MobileNet integration (with dynamic multimodal LLM simulation for ingredient mapping)
*   **Architecture:** 100% Client-side processing for fast, private, and secure interactions.

## 🎯 Target Audience
*   **Eco-Conscious Consumers:** Looking to minimize household carbon footprints.
*   **Budget-Conscious Individuals:** Students and young professionals cutting grocery and takeout costs.
*   **Busy Parents & Home Cooks:** Needing quick inspiration for mismatched leftovers.
*   **Beginner Cooks:** Relying on step-by-step guidance and AI-driven flavor pairings.
